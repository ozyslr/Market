## Overview

Transitioned the live Technical MVP into a full multi-vendor marketplace: built P0
infrastructure (order model, commission engine, immutable ledger, KVKK/GDPR
compliance, Firestore security) then payments, KYC onboarding, search, shipping,
and reviews/trust — in dependency order. Multi-currency (Phase 6) and cross-border
compliance (Phase 8) were deferred to v2; Typesense search (SRC-01/05) rescoped to
v2 with v1 shipping Firestore-based search.
