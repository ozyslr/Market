# Definitions

Canonical definitions used throughout this skill. When in doubt, reference here.

---

## Revenue Terms

**Gross Revenue**
Total sales before any deductions. What the customer paid.

**Net Revenue**
Gross revenue minus returns, refunds, and discounts. This is the "real" top line.

*Shopify note:* Shipping income charged to customers is typically excluded from net revenue in margin calculations. Treat it as an offset to shipping expense, not revenue.

**Returns Rate**
Returns ÷ Gross Revenue. Expressed as percentage.
- Amazon: 1-7% (elite to high)
- Shopify DTC: 2-8% (often higher due to apparel)

---

## Cost Terms

**COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**
Landed cost of products sold during the period. Includes:
- Product cost (FOB or DDP)
- Freight/shipping to warehouse
- Duties/tariffs
- Inspection fees

Does NOT include: fulfillment fees, storage, advertising.

**Landed Cost**
Total cost to get a unit into sellable inventory. COGS per unit.

**Fulfillment Fees (Amazon)**
FBA pick, pack, ship fees. A static dollar amount per unit — does NOT scale with price.

**Fulfillment % of Revenue**
Fulfillment Fees ÷ Net Revenue. Critical because it reveals the fee-to-price trap.

**Storage Fees**
Warehousing costs. Amazon charges monthly based on cubic feet, with Q4 surcharges (3-4× normal rate Oct-Dec).

**Storage % of Revenue**
Monthly storage fees ÷ Net Revenue. Annualized storage would be 12× this figure.

## Margin Terms

**Gross Profit (Traditional)**
Net Revenue − COGS

**Gross Profit (Ecommerce / "After Platform Fees")**
Net Revenue − Referral Fees − Fulfillment Fees − COGS

This is the more useful definition for Amazon sellers because it captures platform costs before you have any discretionary spend.

**Gross Margin %**
Gross Profit ÷ Net Revenue

**Contribution Margin (CM)**
Net Revenue − COGS − Referral Fees − Fulfillment Fees − Storage − Advertising

The profit available to cover fixed costs and generate net income. The single most important metric.

**Contribution Margin % (CM%)**
Contribution Margin ÷ Net Revenue

**SKU-Level CM**
Same calculation, but per-SKU. Requires allocating ad spend to individual products.

**Business-Level CM**
Aggregate across all SKUs and channels. What's left after all variable costs.

## Cash Terms

**Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)**
Days from cash out (paying for inventory) to cash back (collecting from sales).

`CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO`

- **DIO** (Days Inventory Outstanding): How long inventory sits before selling
- **DSO** (Days Sales Outstanding): How long until payment clears (Amazon ~14 days, Shopify ~2-3 days)
- **DPO** (Days Payable Outstanding): How long you can delay paying suppliers

**Cash Floor (Hard)**
Minimum bank balance that triggers emergency action.
`= (Monthly Fixed Costs × 2) + Next Inventory Order Deposit`

**Cash Floor (Soft)**
Target minimum for normal operations.
`= Hard Floor × 1.15 to 1.25`

---

## Advertising Terms

**Ad Spend % of Revenue**
Total advertising spend ÷ Net Revenue

**ACOS (Advertising Cost of Sales)**
Ad Spend ÷ Ad-Attributed Revenue. Amazon-specific metric.

**TACOS (Total ACOS)**
Ad Spend ÷ Total Revenue (not just ad-attributed). More useful for holistic view.

**MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)**
Total Revenue ÷ Total Marketing Spend. The inverse of ad spend %. Higher = more efficient.

---

## Operational Terms

**Velocity**
Units sold per time period (day, week, month). Higher velocity = faster cash cycle.

**Months of Supply**
Current inventory units ÷ Average monthly units sold. Key overstock indicator.

**Days of Supply**
Current inventory units ÷ Average daily units sold.

**SKU Grade**
Classification based on velocity × margin contribution:
- **A**: >5% of sales + healthy CM — prioritize
- **B**: 2-5% of sales, acceptable CM — maintain
- **C**: <2% of sales OR poor CM — evaluate/kill

---

## Channel Terms

**Amazon Referral Fee**
Commission Amazon charges per sale. Typically 15%, varies by category.

**Shopify Payment Processing**
Credit card fees via Shopify Payments or Stripe. Typically ~3%.

**Hybrid Seller**
Operates both Amazon and Shopify (or other DTC). Requires separate P&L analysis per channel.
