## FAQ FOR CEO

**Q: Why $150k? Can we do it cheaper?**  
A: 11-person team × 2 months × $7.5k/person/month = $165k. Services add $17k. We could cut to 8 people ($120k total) but risk missing Week 8 deadline.

**Q: What if we can't hire the Engineering Lead?**  
A: Delay Phase 1 start by 2 weeks. Interim: CEO + most senior dev manage parallel streams.

**Q: Can we skip Seller Subscriptions and go straight to ads?**  
A: No. Ads require seller dashboard infrastructure that subscriptions depend on. Subscriptions unblock ads.

**Q: What's our breakeven on CPC ads?**  
A: Platform takes 25% of ad spend. If $40k monthly ad spend, revenue = $10k/month. Breakeven on team cost = $150k spend to break even in Phase 1.

**Q: Is the Mercora score increase realistic (4.8 → 6.0)?**  
A: Yes. Adding 3 revenue engines + fixing checkout + payment methods = +1.2 points. Conservative estimate.

**Q: Do we need to postpone any existing initiatives?**  
A: Freeze non-critical features during Phase 1. Minimal patches only.
