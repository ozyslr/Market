# Hepsiburada Feature Inventory

## Executive Summary

Hepsiburada (hepsiburada.com) is Turkey's oldest and one of the largest e-commerce platforms, serving ~40 million users. Founded in 1998, it has evolved from a curated electronics retailer to a comprehensive marketplace while maintaining its hallmark focus on product quality, expert curation, and premium services. The platform's competitive strength lies not in pure marketplace scale but in selective merchant partnerships, membership-driven loyalty, and premium customer experiences.

## Unique/Differentiated Features

### SuperPlus Membership Model
- **Free Shipping**: Unlimited free shipping on orders from Partner Merchants
- **Returns Policy**: Free returns up to 60 days (vs. standard 30 days)
- **Exclusive Deals**: Early access to flash sales, bundle discounts, seasonal campaigns
- **Member-Only Discounts**: Dedicated pricing for Super Plus members across select categories
- **Free Return Shipping**: Return label generation and pickup from convenient locations
- **Priority Customer Service**: Dedicated support line, faster response times
- **Cashback/Points**: Earn bonus points on purchases, redeemable for discounts
- **Annual Fee**: Approximately TRY 59-99/year with clear value proposition
- **Digital + Physical Goods**: Works across all categories from electronics to groceries

### Category Specialization & Curation
- **Electronics Mastery**: Deep expertise in smartphones, laptops, tablets, cameras, audio equipment
- **Books Ecosystem**: Partnership with major Turkish publishers, rental program for textbooks
- **Home & Garden**: Curated selection with lifestyle focus, trend-based recommendations
- **Designer Brands**: Official brand stores with verified merchandise and price guarantees
- **Quality Gates**: Fewer 3rd-party sellers with stricter vetting (vs. marketplace competitors)
- **Expert Reviews**: In-house product review team for major items (especially electronics)
- **Buying Guides**: Category-specific guides with expert recommendations, "best of" curations
- **Trending Reports**: Weekly/monthly trend reports showing popular products and emerging categories

### Direct Seller Model & Curation
- **Curated Merchant Base**: Limited number of approved sellers, quality-over-quantity approach
- **Official Brand Stores**: Direct partnerships with manufacturers (Apple, Samsung, Sony, etc.)
- **Verified Seller Badges**: Clear seller verification with brand/reputation indicators
- **Vendor Lock-in**: Exclusivity agreements with premium brands, limited competing listings
- **Commission-Based Model**: Fair-cost marketplace for vetted small businesses
- **Performance Ratings**: Transparent seller metrics (delivery speed, return rate, customer satisfaction)

### Extended Service Programs
- **Price Match Guarantee**: Best price promise on electronics and popular categories
- **Extended Warranties**: 2-5 year protection plans on electronics (beyond manufacturer warranty)
- **Technical Support Warranty**: In-store/remote support for tech products
- **Trade-In Program**: "Eski Cihazını Takas Et" - sell old electronics, get store credit
- **Product Rental Service**: Textbook rental for students, event ticket "rentals" (pay-per-use)
- **Gift Cards**: Physical and digital gift cards, instant delivery option
- **Corporate Sales**: B2B corporate gift program with volume discounts

### Trust & Safety Infrastructure
- **Secure Shopping Badge**: SSL encryption, visible trust indicators on product pages
- **Buyer Protection Guarantee**: Money-back guarantee on disputes, fraud protection
- **Dispute Resolution**: Mediation center with expert review of conflicts
- **Seller Reputation System**: Historical data on seller performance, customer feedback impact
- **Verified Reviews**: Photo/video review requirement for credibility
- **Expert Moderation**: Human review of suspicious or spam reviews
- **Secure Payment Processing**: PCI-DSS compliant, fraud detection algorithms

- **Credit/Debit Card**:
  - Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  - 3D Secure authentication

## Trust & Safety

### Security Infrastructure
- **SSL/TLS Encryption**: HTTPS on all pages (not just checkout)
- **PCI DSS Compliance**: Level 1 certification for card payment handling
- **Fraud Detection System**:
  - Real-time transaction monitoring
  - Machine learning-based anomaly detection
  - Velocity checks (multiple transactions from same card)
  - Geographic verification
  - Device fingerprinting
- **Data Protection**:
  - Encrypted storage of payment information
  - Tokenization of credit cards (no full card storage)
  - GDPR and Turkish Data Protection Law compliance
  - Regular security audits and penetration testing

### Buyer Protection Program
- **Money-Back Guarantee**:
  - If item doesn't arrive, refund within specified timeframe
  - If item not as described, return and refund option
  - If seller never ships, automatic refund after 60 days
- **Fraud Protection**:
  - Unauthorized transaction claim process
  - Card issuer cooperation for chargebacks
  - Zero liability for fraudulent charges (coordinated with banks)
- **Item Protection**:
  - Coverage for lost or damaged packages in transit
  - Insurance coverage coordinated with carriers
  - Photo evidence of damage enables faster refunds
- **Coverage Limits**: Varies by payment method and order amount

### Dispute Resolution Center
- **Escalation Process**:
  1. Direct buyer-seller communication (7 days)
  2. Escalation to Hepsiburada mediation
  3. Expert review by dedicated team
  4. Resolution with financial backing
- **Dispute Categories**:
  - Non-delivery
  - Item not as described
  - Quality/defect issues
  - Return disputes
  - Billing disputes
- **Expert Adjudication**: Case reviewed by specialists (electronics experts for tech, etc.)
- **Decision Timeline**: Typically 7-14 days post-escalation
- **Enforcement**: Hepsiburada refunds buyer if seller won't comply
- **Seller Consequences**: Repeated losses impact seller metrics and account standing

### Seller Verification & Ratings
- **Seller Vetting Process**:
  - Business documentation review
  - Company/individual background check
  - First orders with enhanced monitoring
  - Quality sample inspection (for high-value items)
  - Minimum insurance requirements
- **Seller Performance Tracking**:
  - Real-time metrics dashboard (sellers see their own)
  - Category-specific benchmarking
  - Performance score calculation
  - Automatic flags for performance drops
- **Transparency**:
  - Public seller rating visible to all customers
  - Breakdown of rating dimensions (delivery, communication, etc.)
  - Recent review visibility
  - Seller response rate to customer inquiries
- **Consequence of Poor Performance**:
  - Warning notifications
  - Featured position removal (if applicable)
  - Commission rate increases (proportional penalty)
  - Delisting of products
  - Account suspension/termination

### Review Integrity & Moderation
- **Verification Requirements**:
  - Must be verified purchaser (with rare exceptions for unverified but moderated reviews)
  - Purchase history visible in reviewer profile
  - Photo/video reviews prioritized in ranking
- **Anti-Fraud Measures**:
  - Duplicate review detection (same reviewer, similar text)
  - Competitor review detection (rival company accounts)
  - Astroturfing detection (multiple fake accounts reviewing same product)
  - Sentiment analysis for suspicious patterns
- **Human Moderation**:
  - Flagged reviews reviewed by Hepsiburada team
  - Spam/inappropriate content removal
  - Off-topic review handling
  - Appeals process for removed reviews
- **Review Ranking Algorithm**:
  - Recent reviews weighted higher
  - Helpful vote count as ranking signal
  - Photo/video reviews promoted
  - Detailed reviews ranked above one-word reviews
  - Extreme ratings (all 1-star or all 5-star) reviewed for authenticity
  - Reviewer history/reputation as factor

### Brand Protection
- **Trademark Enforcement**:
  - Brand owner reporting mechanism
  - Counterfeit product removal process
  - Seller IP verification
  - Automatic delisting of suspicious items
- **Authentic Goods Guarantee**:
  - Official brand store partnerships

  - Hologram/authenticity sticker acceptance
- **Warranty Validation**:
  - Seller-provided warranty legitimacy verification

### Privacy & Data Protection
- **Data Collection Transparency**:
  - Clear privacy policy (Turkish & English)
  - Consent management for marketing
  - Cookie disclosure and management

- **User Rights**:
  - Data export functionality
